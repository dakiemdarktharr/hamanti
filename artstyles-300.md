# 300 Art Styles and Aesthetics for Graphic, UI, and Web Design

This is a reference list of visual styles and aesthetic movements that inform graphic design, interfaces, websites, illustration, image-making, and branding. It spans art and design movements, graphic languages, interface aesthetics, rendering styles, photographic treatments, and online aesthetics; these are distinct kinds of labels rather than a single historical taxonomy.

## Research basis and Pinterest collection notes

For the revised scope, the reference base is graphic-design history, interface-design systems, and cultural studies of online aesthetics. The Met and Smarthistory remain useful for art-historical context, while the Smithsonian graphic-design survey, specialist graphic-design collections, official interface documentation, and the internet-aesthetics study better match this applied visual-design vocabulary.

- [Smithsonian Libraries: The Story of Graphic Design](https://www.si.edu/object/story-graphic-design-invention-writing-birth-digital-design-patrick-cramsie:siris_sil_963663) — graphic styles and typography from print to digital.
- [Museum für Gestaltung Zürich: Graphic Design collection](https://museum-gestaltung.ch/en/graphic-design) — European graphic design, lettering, typography, and book design.
- [Tate: Light and Magic](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-modern/light-and-magic/exhibition-guide) — photographic aesthetics and pictorialism.
- [Google Material Design](https://m3.material.io/) — official interface visual system.
- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) — official interface visual language guidance.
- [Microsoft Fluent Design](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/) — official Fluent design system.
- [Giolo and Berghman, “The aesthetics of the self”](https://doi.org/10.5210/fm.v28i3.12723) — peer-reviewed account of internet aesthetics as fluid online labels.
