# UI foundations taxonomy research

This contribution contains 120 entries for the revised handbook. The 35 draft product design systems were removed and replaced with visual aesthetics and relevant art influences. `removed-design-systems.json` preserves the discarded research and is not part of the handbook.

Groups: Typography (45), Composition (31), Surfaces (10), Contemporary aesthetics (21), Color and surface (2), Art influences (11).

The entries distinguish historical art influences, retrospective aesthetic labels, and descriptive visual directions. Type classifications and composition systems are explicitly visual directions, not claimed historical movements. Each entry includes a concise authored description, a distinction from adjacent directions, sources, and exact-name/design/aesthetics reference queries. Vectordelia includes Frutiger Metro as an alias rather than a duplicate entry. Swiss International Style subsumes International Typographic Style; Swiss Punk is handled with New Wave Graphics by the art contribution.

Primary sources include Monotype, Google Fonts, W3C typography documentation, Nielsen Norman Group, Hype4's original surface-design articles, CARI, Tate, V&A, Smithsonian NMAAHC, ACMI, the National Museum of Modern Art Kyoto, James Bridle, and Artie Vierkant. Some retrospective community labels use their originating community documentation. Warm Minimalism, Synthwave and Steampunk retain the original Obsidian handbook as provenance and are not presented as independently formalized art movements.

Pinterest discovery uses the aesthetic name itself, with design/aesthetics disambiguation when necessary. `foundations-hd-discovered.json` records URLs actually returned by TinyFish. No thumbnail URL is rewritten to guess an original. The collector measures decoded native dimensions and requires at least 1920×1080 or 1080×1920. It encodes WebP without resizing. Candidates are not accepted references until visually reviewed in `foundations-hd-approved.json`. Source pages remain labeled pin or board/idea; no individual-pin attribution is invented for a board image. Coverage below eight remains incomplete.

Collection and visual approval checkpoints are stored separately so unfinished discovery cannot become false completed coverage. Local staging has a shared 1 GiB guard; Mongo upload selection remains independently budgeted.

Exa search accounting: 340 requested results across all searches for this contribution. Fetch calls and TinyFish collection calls are excluded from this count.

## Final review checkpoint

All 220 previously approved references were visually re-reviewed against the people policy: 199 remained accepted and 21 were excluded. A final pass then reviewed all 71 already-measured pending candidate records, accepting 31 and rejecting or declining 40. Six of those rejections concerned casual or candid photographs. No new crawling was performed in that final pass.

The final contribution contains 230 accepted references: 198 without photographed real people and 32 with deliberate model/editorial/product posing. Every accepted record has a peopleReview flag. foundations-people-review.json preserves the combined audit; foundations-cached-final-review.json isolates the final 71-record pass.

Of 120 categories, 6 have at least eight accepted images, 73 have partial coverage and 41 have none. The remaining deficit is 731 images after capping each category at eight. The eight-image target is not complete. All measured candidates are reviewed. Discovery is paused for publication, not exhausted: 126 discovery rows were added after the latest measurement pass. Full counts and source limitations are in foundations-hd-coverage.json. No tests were added or run.
