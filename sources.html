References & credits

This edition contains 300 web and interface aesthetics, visual directions, and art influences. Each entry links to its sources. The original Obsidian handbook remains available below.

References are checked for relevance and native Full HD dimensions: at least 1920 × 1080, or 1080 × 1920 for portraits. Small images are excluded rather than enlarged. “Examples pending” means fewer than six reviewed images are available.

Photography of people is limited to deliberately posed model or editorial imagery. Casual group photos, selfies and candid people shots are excluded. Illustrated figures are welcome.

The collection is being selected by hand. Reviewed images appear as they are added; empty entries are still being prepared.

Source links are shown where supplied. “Selected reference” identifies a supplied image without an accompanying source link. Images belong to their respective creators.

Motion and Lenis power the interactions. The typewriter, curtain, line reveal, scroll gallery, introduction, and footer are independent implementations inspired by the linked Motion examples; no Motion+ source is included.

The lightweight SVG backgrounds are independently drawn interpretations of the topography, ASCII, wave, beam, and shard directions suggested by React Bits. One pattern and palette remain fixed throughout each style visit.

Fonts: Syne, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Mono, and Manrope, self-hosted through Fontsource.

The Canva component sheet informed the controls and imperfect h-star mark. UI graphics are SVG vectors.

The horizontal landscape uses Shan Shui Inf by Lingdong Huang, copyright 2018, under the MIT license. Its SVG generator runs in a background worker, with the original grayscale washes recolored to hamanti’s dark green. The mountains are decorative scenery, not reference examples.
