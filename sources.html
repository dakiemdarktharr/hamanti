References & credits

The 300 style names, descriptions, and eight groups come from the original Obsidian handbook. The descriptions are unchanged.

The gallery displays up to eight collected references for each style. “Examples pending” means fewer than eight have been selected. A full set describes coverage, not independent authentication of the artwork.

Pinterest pins and boards retain their original links. Image and board titles come from the source results. Board images do not imply that the board owner created the artwork. Images belong to their respective creators.

The original collection and its historical coverage remain available below. Current coverage includes the resumed collection.

Motion and Lenis power the interactions. The typewriter, curtain, line reveal, scroll gallery, introduction, and footer are independent implementations inspired by the linked Motion examples; no Motion+ source is included.

The reference background is Topography by React Bits, copyright David Haz, used under its MIT + Commons Clause license. Its colors are fixed for the duration of each style visit. Where available, palettes are extracted from a recorded reference; documented editorial colors cover unavailable images.

Fonts: Syne, Instrument Serif, IBM Plex Mono, and Manrope, self-hosted through Fontsource. The botanical line drawings are original interface illustrations, not artwork references.
